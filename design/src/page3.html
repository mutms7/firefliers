Shading

Rules

One pixel size per familyCreatures 1.85, bosses 3.6 world units per pixel. Never mixed.
Light from the top leftFive-step ramps. Shadows lean purple, highlights lean warm.
Dark outlineOne pixel, darker than sprite and ground. Gold for elites.
Flip, don't rotateCreatures face right and flip to face you.
Two framesWalk, flap, flicker. Keep idle motion small.
Stations get their own lightDarker between them, so they stand out.
