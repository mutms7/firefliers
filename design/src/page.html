The Hollow hub

A sheltered nook under the roots of an old tree at the meadow's edge, where the fireflies rest by day. You walk around it between runs. Lit areas work today; the three foggy corners are locked, with a padlocked gate and a coming-soon board, ready for new modes and merchants.

    Characters

    Your five bugs as pixel sprites to match the enemies, the two merchants, and the fireflies. Locked bugs sit on the Roost as shadows.

    Hub props and tiles

    Warmer and greener than the run, because this is home. Tiles are 16 by 16 with clusters that wrap across their edges so the grid doesn't show.
